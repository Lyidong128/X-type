Low-energy 2x2 projection near M
================================
Projection basis columns P = Matrix([[1/2, 1/2], [1/2, 1/2], [1/2, -1/2], [1/2, -1/2]])
H_eff_M = Matrix([[2*t + v - w, 0], [0, -v + w]])
d0 = t
dx = 0
dy = 0
dz = t + v - w
m_M(v,t,w) = dz = t + v - w
critical condition m_M=0 => -t + w
